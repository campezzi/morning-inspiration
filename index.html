Miura P400 SV
Marcello Gandini / Bertone — 1971

The V12 sits sideways behind the seats, which is why the whole car can be this low and this short in the nose. Gandini was in his twenties when Bertone drew it, and the headlight surrounds are lashed with slats like eyelashes, half-closed, as if the car were squinting into speed. The SV was the last and wildest of the line, with wider rear haunches and about 385 horsepower from four litres. Owners of the early cars reported the front end going light above a certain pace. Nobody seems to have slowed down because of it.
