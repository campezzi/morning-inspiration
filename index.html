Beware of Luxury
Jan Steen — 1663

A slate hangs on the wall reading "in weelde siet toe" — beware of luxury — and not one person in the room is looking at it. A pig noses through the spilled dinner. A child is handed a pipe like it's candy. Someone's stocking has come loose, someone else has passed out over the lute, and a monkey has gotten into the clock. Steen painted his own household this disorderly on purpose; "a Jan Steen household" became Dutch shorthand for a mess long before art historians got hold of it.
