Music Has the Right to Children
Boards of Canada — 1998
Listen: Music Has the Right to Children — Boards of Canada

The record sounds like a home movie left on a windowsill for twenty years: the colour has gone out of it in patches, and the tape wobbles where someone's thumb once touched the reel. Boards of Canada made it in 1998 at their own studio, Hexagon Sun, in the Pentland Hills, out of vintage synthesisers, found sounds and samples they let degrade on purpose.

Children's voices drift through it, too far away to make out. The tunes are gentle, almost nursery-bright, and that is what hurts, because the afternoon they remember is already over and nobody can say when it ended.
