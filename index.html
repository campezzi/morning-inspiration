Messerschmitt KR200
Fritz Fend — 1955

The KR200 has a fighter's canopy and a lawnmower's ambitions. It hinges open sideways, the way a cockpit would, and inside two people sit in tandem, one behind the other, steering with handlebars instead of a wheel. Fritz Fend designed it, and Messerschmitt built it because the company had been banned from making aircraft after the war. You can see where the habit went.
