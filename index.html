Cuadra San Cristóbal
Luis Barragán — 1968

A single jet of water falls from a rust-red wall into a pool the colour of glass, and nothing else in the courtyard moves. Past the bougainvillea, the pink walls rise higher than a rider on horseback, so the sky is the only window and the water the only sound. Barragán built the stables and the house for the Egerstrøm family in the 1960s, and he gave the horses a place that looks like it was made for a long silence. The pool's edge is a thin white line, as if the water had been poured to its brim and then left to settle.
