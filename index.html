Cubo Ashtray
Bruno Munari — 1957

Munari molded it to look like a square of paper with one corner peeled back — except it's rigid melamine, and the peel is a slot, and cigarette butts vanish through it into a bent aluminum baffle where you can't see them anymore. No lid, no hinge, no ash on display: the whole idea was to hide the mess instead of putting it in a dish. He later admitted, in a 1992 lecture, that he'd gotten the psychology backwards — people like watching an ashtray fill up, and his hid its own purpose too well. Danese took three years to agree to manufacture it. By then it had talked itself out of being useful and into being a very good joke.
