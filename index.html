Macintosh Icons
Susan Kare — 1984

Susan Kare drew every one of these on graph paper, one square per pixel, in a grid thirty-two across and thirty-two down. The trash can on the right edge of the screen has a lid and a handle and a little dent in its side, which is more character than most people give their actual bins. She also gave the machine a smiling face for when it started up, a bomb for when it didn't, and a looped-square symbol for the Command key that she borrowed from a Swedish sign for campgrounds and places of interest. Forty years of computers have followed her lead, and the bin is still a bin.
