A Wild and Crazy Guy
Steve Martin — 1978
Listen: A Wild and Crazy Guy — Steve Martin

Side one was recorded in a small San Francisco nightclub; side two, a few months later, at Red Rocks in front of thousands. You can hear the room change size mid-record — Martin reads a fake financial report of his own earnings, and by the time he gets to the number the intimate club laughter has become an amphitheater roar. He does a bit where he reveals his "real name" is just him flapping his lips. The album closes with "King Tut," a novelty single about ancient Egypt's most famous mummy that somehow reached the Billboard Hot 100. None of it explains itself, and none of it needs to.
