X-15A-2
North American Aviation — 1967

The white coating along the fuselage is scorched dark in long streaks, blistered where the heat worked its way underneath — this is what the airplane looked like when it came back. On October 3, 1967, Air Force Major William "Pete" Knight flew the X-15A-2 to Mach 6.70, 4,520 miles an hour, faster than anyone has ever taken a piloted aircraft. The dummy ramjet slung under the fuselage partly melted away, and the heat burned holes clean through the lower fin. The airplane never flew again. Fifty-nine years on, nothing with a person in it has gone faster.
