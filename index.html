Donkey Kong
Shigeru Miyamoto / Nintendo — 1981
Read: Donkey Kong — Shigeru Miyamoto / Nintendo

Nintendo wanted a Popeye game, couldn't get the licence, and handed a young industrial designer named Shigeru Miyamoto the leftovers. He kept the triangle (a big brute, a damsel, a rival) and swapped in a gorilla, a carpenter and a stack of crooked girders. The hero was just called Jumpman, and he got his famous name only after someone noticed he looked like the landlord of the warehouse.

Look at the cabinet and you can see how little it takes: a few bold colours, a name in comic lettering, an ape that is clearly pleased with himself. It is a game about a very bad day at a building site, and people queued up for it with quarters.
