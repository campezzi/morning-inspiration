Saturn Eclipse
NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute (Cassini) — 2006

Cassini slipped into Saturn's shadow and turned to look back at the sun, and the planet becomes a black disc rimmed with a thin bright line of atmosphere. The rings, lit from behind, glow like dust in a window: the narrow F ring, the faint outer E ring, a blue-ish D ring close in. Left of the planet, small and easy to miss, is Earth, a pale speck between two rings. Everyone anyone has ever known is in that speck, and the picture doesn't mention it. The colours in this version are pushed beyond what the eye would see, but the quiet is not.
