The Nightmare
Henry Fuseli — 1781

A woman lies across the bed with her head and arms hanging off the edge, as if she were dropped there. On her stomach sits a small grey creature with its eyes on us, not on her; behind the curtain a horse with blank, white eyes leans into the room. Nothing in the picture moves, but the whole thing feels held mid-fall.

Fuseli showed it at the Royal Academy in 1782 and people queued, and he painted it again for years afterwards. The horse is the part I can't settle: it has no business in a bedroom, and it looks the least bothered of anyone present.
