St. Petri Church, Klippan
Sigurd Lewerentz — 1966

Lewerentz set one rule for his bricklayers: no brick would be cut. Every arch and reveal at St. Petri had to resolve in whole units, so the coursing rises and dips and the mortar smears wide across the joints wherever a fraction wouldn't fit. He was eighty-one when the church was consecrated, in 1966, after more than a decade away from professional practice. The nave floor tilts toward the altar and finally cracks open into a well beneath a great conch shell — the font, fed by water that never stops moving. Two corten-steel beams cross overhead at an off-center column, holding up the brick vault like a spine.
