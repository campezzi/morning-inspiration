The Trial of the Sow at Lavegny
Burgundian court records, via Chambers's Book of Days — 1457

In 1457 a sow and her six piglets were hauled before a Burgundian court, accused of killing a child and eating part of it. The mother was found guilty and sentenced to death, which is at least consistent. The piglets were acquitted on grounds of their youth, the bad example set by their mother, and the lack of direct proof that they'd joined in. Somewhere a clerk wrote down "bad example" and moved on to the next case.
