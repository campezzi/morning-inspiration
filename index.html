1955 Mille Miglia
Stirling Moss — 1955

Jenkinson wrote the route down before the race — eight metres of paper on a hand-cranked scroll, notes for every curve on 992 miles of Italian public road. The Mercedes' engine was too loud to hear him, so he switched to hand signals over the noise: a tap on Moss's leg to tighten the line, a flat palm to open it out. They finished in 10 hours, 7 minutes, 48 seconds — 32 minutes ahead of Fangio in an identical car, at an average of 99 mph that has stood as a record since. Two years later the race was banned from open roads after a crash killed nine spectators, so nobody has had the chance to beat it.
