Fangio Chasing Collins, German Grand Prix
Willy Pragher — 1957

The grandstand behind them is a smear — the shutter stayed open too long for how fast these two are moving. That's Fangio in the near Maserati, hunting down Peter Collins' Ferrari on the pit straight at the Nürburgring, having just lost fifty-two seconds to a botched wheel change that dropped him almost a minute behind the leaders. Over the laps that followed he broke his own lap record nine times, ended up running twenty-four seconds quicker than anyone had gone all weekend, and clawed back that deficit on a fourteen-mile circuit that leaves nowhere to hide a mistake. He passed Collins, then Hawthorn, and won by 3.6 seconds — the fastest, by his own account, he ever drove a car in his life.
