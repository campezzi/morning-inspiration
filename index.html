The Drawbridge
Giovanni Battista Piranesi — 1761

The drawbridge hangs over nothing — no far bank, no landing, just chains running down into arches that recede farther than the eye can resolve. Piranesi reworked this plate for the 1761 edition, thickening the crosshatching and adding a second staircase that climbs to a floor that isn't there. Despite the title, no prisoners appear in it. The architecture does the confining on its own: too many stairs, not enough exits, a structure built for a purpose the print never states.
