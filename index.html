Paris Street; Rainy Day
Gustave Caillebotte — 1877

Twenty-four people cross a wet junction in the Place de Dublin, and not one of them looks at another. The couple in front, sharing an umbrella, stare past the edge of the frame at something we are never shown; a lamppost splits the canvas down the middle, and the cobbles hold the grey sky like a second sheet of weather.

Haussmann's new boulevards are so clean here that they feel unlived in. Everyone is close enough to touch, and the umbrellas make sure nobody does.
