A Page of the Voynich Manuscript
Unknown scribe — c. 1420

The page is mostly handwriting, steady and unhurried, in an alphabet that no one has been able to read. Behind the lines, a few green leaf shapes have been washed in and seem to float without stems, as if the plant they belonged to was removed and the paint left behind. Two red initials sit in the margin where a reader would expect a paragraph to begin. Whoever wrote this knew exactly what it said; the vellum has been carbon-dated to the early fifteenth century, and the book has kept that knowledge to itself ever since.
