Moanin'
Art Blakey and the Jazz Messengers — 1958
Read: Moanin' — Art Blakey and the Jazz Messengers

It opens with a call and a reply: Bobby Timmons's piano states a phrase, and the horns answer it like a congregation that can't wait its turn. Then Blakey's drums start shoving the whole band forward, snare cracks landing just ahead of where you expect them. Timmons wrote the tune in a church-shaped hurry, and Benny Golson's arrangement keeps every bar leaning into the next. The photograph is from The Hague five years later, the band in a concert hall, Blakey hunched over the kit as if the song were still pulling him along.
