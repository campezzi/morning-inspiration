Melancholy
Edvard Munch — 1892

A man sits on a black beach, head propped in his hand, while a couple walks out toward a yellow boat waiting at the end of a jetty. Munch based him on his friend Jappe Nilssen, who was in love with a married woman that summer at Åsgårdstrand — but he gave the friend his own earlier heartbreak too, so the figure carries two men's griefs and neither face. The shoreline bends away from him in one unbroken stroke, dragging the eye toward the boat whether it wants to go or not. He painted the scene five times over five years, unable to leave the beach either.
