La Huida
Remedios Varo — 1961

This is only the bottom of the painting: a vessel lined in pale fur, its torn sail trailing, riding on a sea of gold dust toward grey mountains. Above the rim, out of frame, a figure in a blue cloak stands on legs that look woven rather than grown. Varo painted in egg tempera, one hair at a time, and you can count the strokes in the hull.

The boat has no oars, only that single ragged fin and a few threads pulled taut. It moves, but nothing says where, or what it is fleeing, or whether the dust is a sea at all. La Huida is from 1961, two years before she died.
