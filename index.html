Giant Solar Eruption
Solar Orbiter (ESA/NASA) — 2022

On 15 February 2022, a prominence tore loose from the Sun's surface and arced roughly 3.5 million kilometres out — five solar radii, enough room to line up ten Earths along its length. Solar Orbiter's Extreme Ultraviolet Imager caught the whole thing in a single frame, eruption and disc together, closer than any spacecraft had managed before. The plasma was moving away from Earth; aimed the other way, the geomagnetic storm would have made headlines for different reasons. No warning flare, no slow build — the surface held, then it didn't.

Source: European Space Agency
