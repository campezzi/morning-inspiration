Broke, baby sick, and car trouble!
Dorothea Lange — 1937

The caption is the family's own words: broke, baby sick, and car trouble. Dorothea Lange found them stopped on the shoulder of U.S. 99 outside Tracy, California, in February 1937, seven months out from a Missouri drought that had already taken everything else. The car behind them is loaded past the roofline — mattresses, coiled wire, a washtub, all roped down against however many miles were still ahead. The mother kneels on a quilt spread over the gravel, one hand pressed to her face; the baby sits beside her in a knit bonnet, not yet old enough to know what's wrong. Lange rarely titled her Farm Security Administration pictures — this one already had one, spoken by the people in it.
