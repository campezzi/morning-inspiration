Pac-Man
Toru Iwatani / Namco — 1980
Read: Pac-Man — Namco

The cabinet is a yellow wedge of a thing, and its side art is a Pac-Man with arms, legs and a jaunty stride, nothing like the armless disc on the screen. Toru Iwatani said the shape came from a pizza with one slice gone, which is the kind of origin story you'd distrust if the game weren't so plainly built from it.

He wanted something that wouldn't scare off people who didn't play arcade games, so he gave it ghosts with names, a fruit bonus, and a reason to run away rather than a reason to shoot. Each ghost chases you a little differently. Pinky ambushes, Clyde loses his nerve, and everyone loses a quarter.
