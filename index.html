Carta Marina
Olaus Magnus — 1539

Off Norway a sea serpent longer than the ship it's attacking coils through the water, mid-strike, forever. Olaus Magnus filled the sea around Scandinavia with things like it rather than leave the water blank — a lobster large enough to drag a man under, a whale mistaken for an island and lit with a cooking fire by sailors who didn't know better, a boar-faced fish grinning up from the deep. He was a Swedish churchman exiled by the Reformation, drawing the coastline of a home he could no longer visit from memory, secondhand reports, and old sailors' tales. The land holds still and behaves; the water is where he let himself go.
