Jim Clark, Lotus 25, German Grand Prix
Jim Clark — 1962

Clark sits low in the Lotus 25, elbows tucked in, the car so narrow he seems to be wearing it. This is the Nürburgring in 1962, twenty-two kilometres of crests and blind dips that most drivers spent a season learning to fear. The 25 was the first Formula One car built around a monocoque tub, and it made everything before it look like furniture.

Nothing in the frame is slow. The blur isn't the camera's fault; the photographer has just caught the instant before the car is gone.
