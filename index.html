Environs of Paris
Eugène Atget — 1920s

One tree takes up almost the whole plate, its trunk splitting into a dozen limbs that thin out to a blur of twigs at the edges, where the wind moved them during the exposure. Behind it the park is empty: lawn, a few more bare trees, a low line of woods. Atget spent decades photographing Paris and its edges for anyone who might want a record, and sold prints for a few francs; here there is no building and no person, only the season and a long, patient look. It is late afternoon, and the shadows have already reached the grass.
