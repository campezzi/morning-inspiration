Space Invaders
Tomohiro Nishikado — 1978

Tomohiro Nishikado built the hardware himself and then found the aliens were too fast for it to be fair: as invaders were shot, the machine had fewer of them to draw, so the ones left simply sped up. He kept it. What reads as cruelty in the design is an accident of a processor running out of chores, and it made the game feel like a tide coming in. This is the cocktail version, the screen lying flat under glass like a tray of drinks, meant to be played across a bar table with a friend. Japan supposedly ran short of 100-yen coins in 1978, though the shortage may be a story that grew larger than the coins.
