The Painter and His Pug
William Hogarth — 1745

Hogarth has painted himself as an oval portrait propped on a stack of books, with Shakespeare, Milton and Swift underneath, and his palette lying on the ledge with a single serpentine line drawn across it. The pug, Trump, sits in the foreground looking rather more solid than the man. Hogarth has put his dog in the lower corner, which is where a signature would normally go.

The face is firm, scarred over one eye, and plainly wants to be taken seriously, though the dog gets there first.
