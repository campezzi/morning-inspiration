Water Croquet
Jean-Marc Côté and others — 1899

Two players in diving dress stand on the seabed, mallets raised, while hoops are set out in the sand as on any lawn in Surrey. A fish drifts through the wicket and no one minds. The card is one of a set of fifty issued around 1900 as cigarette-box and postcard inserts, picturing the year 2000 with the confidence of people who had just been handed electricity.

The oddest part is how little the artist needed to invent: the croquet, the etiquette, the stance are all unchanged, only the water is new. The future here is the present with something wrong about the air.
