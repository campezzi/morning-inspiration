The Driving Instructor
Bob Newhart — 1960
Listen: The Driving Instructor — Bob Newhart

The joke is that we only hear one side of it. Newhart plays a driving instructor stuck in a car with a Mrs. Webb, and every silence has to carry her incompetence for her — a flashing red light, a school crossing, whatever it is that keeps nearly getting them both killed. He recorded it live at the Tidelands Club in Houston in February 1960, and his voice never once rises; it just gets more carefully polite, which is worse. The album beat Sinatra, Nat King Cole, and Belafonte for Album of the Year at the 1961 Grammys — the first comedy record to take it, and still the bestselling comedy album of the century. Nobody laughs on the recording as hard as they laugh at how long he can hold the panic in.
