War of the Worlds
Frank R. Paul — 1927

Frank R. Paul drew three Martian tripods stalking over a burning town for the August 1927 cover of Amazing Stories, illustrating H.G. Wells three decades after War of the Worlds was first serialized. He gives the machines the same patient linework as the crowd fleeing beneath them — strutwork, rivets, the diagonal beams where their heat-rays are already firing. Paul had no photograph to work from and no precedent for a Martian anything; the confidence in the drawing is the whole argument. It's the cover where the invaders finally look like they could win.
