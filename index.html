Moebius (Jean Giraud) — 1975

A helmeted, caped rider crosses a landscape of red rock on a heavyset, pterodactyl-like creature, and the strip never explains who he is or where he's going. Jean Giraud drew the four wordless stories in 1975 under a new name, Moebius — a break from Gir, the pseudonym he'd used for a decade on the Western series Lieutenant Blueberry. The ink avoids straight lines and hard points wherever it can; even the cliffs curve like something breathing. Métal Hurlant ran the strips in France that year, and Heavy Metal reprinted them in its first American issue in 1977 — still no dialogue, no captions, nothing to translate. Each story drops the rider into the same rock formations and never lets him find a way out of them.
