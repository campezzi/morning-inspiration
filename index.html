Shadows
John Cassavetes — 1959

Cassavetes shot the first version on borrowed 16mm stock with no script and no permits, just actors from his workshop turned loose on Manhattan streets. He screened it once, in 1958, decided it felt too written, and reshot most of it. Charles Mingus was hired to score it but kept writing songs that stood on their own instead of following the picture, so what survives on the soundtrack is mostly saxophonist Shafi Hadi improvising while Cassavetes acted out the missing scenes for him in the booth. The camera never quite settles either — it drifts off a speaker mid-sentence, catches the backs of heads, restless in exactly the way the story is.
