L'Eclisse
Michelangelo Antonioni — 1962

Vittoria and Piero agree, almost offhandedly, to meet again that evening at their usual corner in Rome's EUR district. Antonioni holds that corner for the last seven minutes of the film — the same street sweeper, the same water barrel filling from a leaking cask, the same fading light — and neither of them ever shows up. Monica Vitti has spent the preceding ninety minutes circling an affair she can't quite enter and can't quite leave; the city simply goes on without her. It's the coldest ending in Antonioni's alienation trilogy because nothing, technically, happens in it at all.
