The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari
Robert Wiene — 1920

A man in a top hat and round spectacles walks down a street that has no right angles. The walls lean in, the pavement is a scatter of painted cracks, and the shadows are brushed on the flats rather than cast by any light. Hermann Warm, Walter Reimann and Walter Röhrig built the whole town this way, in a Berlin studio, out of canvas and black paint.

Caligari himself stands almost still, which makes the street look like the thing that is moving. The film eventually tells you it was all a madman's story, but the set never agrees. It keeps tilting after the explanation.
