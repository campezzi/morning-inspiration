A Little Fable
Franz Kafka — 1920
"You only need to change your direction," said the cat, and ate it up.
— Franz Kafka, A Little Fable (1920)

A mouse tells the cat its whole life story in one breath: the world used to be so big it was frightening, then the walls it was grateful for kept narrowing, and now here it is in the last room, staring at the trap in the corner. It never occurs to the mouse that the walls and the trap were always the same project. The cat doesn't explain any of this. It just gives the only advice that finishes the story. Kafka needed about ninety words and didn't need to raise his voice once.
