Serio-Comic War Map for the Year 1877
Fred W. Rose — 1877

Fred W. Rose drew the Russian Empire as an octopus, tentacles reaching out across the continent toward whatever border happened to be exposed. He filed it under "serio-comic," which was the polite way of admitting the joke was doing actual political work: the map was built to sow distrust of Russia across Europe, dressed up as a punchline. Rose kept at it — a comic map of the 1880 British election, another octopus in 1899 called Angling in Troubled Waters — as if satire were just cartography with the pretense removed. The tentacles are drawn with the same care as the coastlines. Nobody told the engraver which parts were supposed to be funny.
