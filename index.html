Interior in Strandgade, Sunlight on the Floor
Vilhelm Hammershøi — 1901

A woman sits with her back to the room, occupied with something in her lap that the painting never explains. This is Ida, Hammershøi's wife, in their apartment at Strandgade 30 in Copenhagen — a set of rooms he returned to again and again, rearranging the furniture, the light, the hour, but rarely the mood. The palette runs almost entirely gray and white, broken only by the plane of sunlight crossing the floorboards toward the far wall. He never once shows what's outside the window. Nothing here asks to be looked at twice, which is exactly why it holds.

Source: SMK — National Gallery of Denmark
