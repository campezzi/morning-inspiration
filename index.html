Boogie Stop Shuffle
Charles Mingus — 1959
Listen: Boogie Stop Shuffle — Charles Mingus

Twelve-bar blues underneath, but the bass line won't sit still — it keeps lurching from stop-time into a full shuffle and back, like a car braking and accelerating through traffic. Mingus cut it at Columbia's 30th Street Studio in May 1959, one of nine tracks Teo Macero had to trim down to fit a single LP side. Dannie Richmond's drums chase the bass the whole way, never quite catching it. Nothing here resolves so much as it keeps moving until the track just stops.
