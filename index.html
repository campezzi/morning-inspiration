Iso / Ermenegildo Preti — 1953

The whole front of the car is the door. It swings open with the steering wheel attached, so you step in off the pavement and the dashboard follows you like a loyal dog. Ermenegildo Preti drew it for Iso, the Italian refrigerator maker, in 1953, and it looks like something the refrigerators dreamed up on their night off.

BMW bought the tooling and swapped in a single-cylinder motorcycle engine, and the egg went on to keep the company solvent through the lean years. The rear wheels sit so close together that it needs no differential, which is the kind of engineering joke that only works when it's also true.

Photo via Wikimedia Commons · Iso Autoveicoli, 1953; built under licence by BMW from 1955
