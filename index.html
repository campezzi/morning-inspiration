Bagsværd Church
Jørn Utzon — 1976

The ceiling is poured concrete shaped like cloud, and it sags and lifts over the nave as if breathing. Utzon sketched it after watching clouds over a beach, and the light that slides down the curves from hidden clerestories changes the white from grey to blue to something close to warm.

Below it everything is small and modest: pale tile, plain wooden pews, an organ in a wooden case. The building is a Danish suburban church, and it asks you to look up and stay a while.
