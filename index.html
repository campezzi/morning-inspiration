The War of the Bucket
Modena — 1325

One night in 1325, a raiding party from Modena climbed into Bologna and made off with the only thing worth stealing at that hour: a wooden well-bucket. Bologna took it as a declaration of war — and got one. The fighting that followed left roughly two thousand men dead, and at the end of it Modena still had the bucket, so it stayed, mounted on permanent, deliberately taunting display, where it still hangs seven hundred years later. Bologna, for what it's worth, has never quite let it go.
