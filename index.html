Ronnie Peterson, Lotus 72, Silverstone
Gillfoto (photo of Ronnie Peterson) — 1975

The Lotus 72 is a wedge with its nose down and its wings up, painted black and gold, and Ronnie Peterson is sitting in it as if the corner were already behind him. Silverstone, 1975: the car is by now old enough to be somebody's grandfather, six seasons on from its debut, and still he drags it sideways through the turns while newer machines sit politely in line.

Peterson drove like that all the time, wheels crossed up, never lifting, never apologising to the tyres. Hold the picture a second longer and the blur says the rest: the car is gone before the shutter closes, and so, three years on at Monza, is he.
