The Walking Bed
Winsor McCay — 1908

Every Sunday for six years, Nemo's dream ended the same way: he fell out of bed and woke up. Here McCay turns the running gag on itself — the bed grows legs of its own, six panels of impossible anatomy, and carries Nemo through a city that tilts and buckles to match its stride. It ran in the New York Herald on 26 July 1908, one strip among hundreds. By the last panel Nemo is on the floor of his own room, same as always, and nothing about the walk has been explained.
