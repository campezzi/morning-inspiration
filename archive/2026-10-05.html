Leo Tolstoy at Yasnaya Polyana
Sergey Prokudin-Gorsky — 1908

Tolstoy stands among the trees at his estate in a dark coat, white beard against the green, one hand tucked into his belt. He was eighty, two years from the night he would leave this house for good. The picture is a colour one, made by exposing three glass plates in turn through red, green and blue filters, and the old man had to hold still for all three.

He does, and the tiny misalignments at the edges of the leaves are the only part of the scene that moved.
