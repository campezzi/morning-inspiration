The Eye, Like a Strange Balloon, Mounts Towards Infinity
Odilon Redon — 1882

The balloon's envelope is an eye, lashes fanned out like static, pupil fixed on nothing in particular. Underneath it hangs a gondola, and inside the gondola, on a second look, is a skull. Redon drew this as the first plate in a set of six lithographs dedicated to Poe, a writer he knew only secondhand, through Baudelaire's French. Below, the sea flattens into a dark, indifferent band, and a stand of marsh grass in the corner holds its ground while everything else keeps rising.
