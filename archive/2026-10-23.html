Chet Baker Sings
Chet Baker — 1954
Listen: Chet Baker Sings — Chet Baker

Chet Baker was a trumpeter first, and the singing arrived almost as an afterthought: a small, unvibratoed voice with hardly any range, set down on Pacific Jazz in 1954. It barely rises off the floor. On "I Fall in Love Too Easily" he sounds less like a man performing the line than one admitting to it, and the trumpet, when it comes in, plays the same sentence over again without the words.

"My Funny Valentine" is here too, the version he would carry through every set until the end. He kept coming back to these songs for decades, and they never got any happier.
