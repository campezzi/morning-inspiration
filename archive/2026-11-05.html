Man with a Movie Camera
Dziga Vertov — 1929

The camera tilts up the curve of an apartment tower, balcony over balcony, until the spire cuts into a flat white sky. Vertov shot it in Moscow, Kyiv and Odesa, and the film has no actors, no intertitles and no story. It is a day in the city, cut so fast that trams, factory looms, a woman waking and a man at a hand-cranked camera all arrive on top of each other.

Some shots last a second or less, and a few are doubled, tripled, run backwards or frozen mid-stride. The building in this frame holds still, which makes it the odd one out. You can almost feel the cut coming.
