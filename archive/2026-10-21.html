Kambara: Evening Snow
Utagawa Hiroshige — 1834

Snow falls on a village at night, and the sky above it is printed in a gradient that goes from black to nothing. Two travellers hunch under straw capes and one walks with his head tucked down, the only figures awake. The houses are shuttered, their roofs heavy and white, and there is no sound on the page at all. Hiroshige is said to have never seen snow fall at Kambara, which lies on a coast where it rarely settles; the quiet may be borrowed from somewhere else.
