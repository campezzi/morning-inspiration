Addie Card, 12 Years. Spinner in North Pownal Cotton Mill
Lewis Hine — 1910

She stands at the frame's edge in a dark, shapeless dress, one hand resting on the machinery she works, looking at the camera without performing for it. Lewis Hine wrote on the caption that the girls in the mill said Addie was ten; she admitted to him that she was twelve, and had started during school vacation and now would "stay." The frame is so plain it barely argues, and the long rows of bobbins behind her recede into a blur that says the same thing. Her face has the patience of someone who has already stopped expecting the question to matter.
