Autobahn
Kraftwerk — 1974
Listen: Autobahn — Kraftwerk

It opens with a car door slamming, an engine turning over, then twenty-two minutes of a four-note pulse that doesn't build toward anything — it just holds the speed. Kraftwerk recorded it in the summer of 1974, splitting time between Conny Plank's farmhouse studio and their own Kling Klang space in Düsseldorf, layering real engine noise under synthesized whooshes meant to sound like cars overtaking on the motorway. They always said the ignition sound at the start was their own car; a stock sound-effects record later turned up with the identical take. Emil Schult's cover shows the road itself — a Volkswagen and a Mercedes shrinking into gray distance under a sign that gives no destination. The single edit cut the whole thing to three minutes and it still charted; the album version never resolves, it just keeps driving.
