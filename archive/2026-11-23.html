Lancia Stratos HF Stradale
Marcello Gandini / Bertone for Lancia — 1973

The nose is a flat wedge, the windscreen a wraparound bubble, and the whole car is shorter than a Mini Cooper with the wheels shoved out to the corners. Marcello Gandini drew it at Bertone to do one thing, win rallies, and the doors, glass and proportions all follow from that. The wheelbase is so short that it twitches under you; drivers described it as a car that wants to turn before you've asked.

Lancia built roughly 492 road versions so the Stratos could be homologated, then won the World Rally Championship three years running, 1974 to 1976. This one is parked on a lawn among tents and strangers, and still looks like it's leaning into a corner.
