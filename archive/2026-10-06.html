The Fire Channels of the Earth
Athanasius Kircher — 1665

Kircher cuts the Earth open like a fruit and finds it wired. A central fire sits at the core, and from it channels branch outward through the rock, each ending in a volcano on the surface. It is neat, almost municipal, a plumbing diagram for something that has no business having plumbing.

He published it in 1665, after being lowered into the crater of Vesuvius, and the engraving looks as though what he saw down there has been tidied into a system. The mountains are meant to be chimneys. You keep wondering who maintains the pipes.
