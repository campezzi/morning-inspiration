Concorde G-BOAF, Main Cabin
British Aircraft Corporation / Aérospatiale (photo: Alan Wilson) — 1978

Four narrow seats to a row, two on each side, and a ceiling you could touch from the aisle. The windows are the size of a paperback, and the cabin is empty and evenly lit, the way a room looks when the party has been gone a while.

G-BOAF made 5,639 supersonic flights and landed at Filton on 26 November 2003, the last flight any Concorde ever made. She now sits in a hangar in Bristol, a few miles from where she was built. The seats still face forward.
