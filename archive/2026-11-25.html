About the album: Rain Dogs — Tom Waits

It opens on a marimba and a clatter of junk percussion, and then Waits comes in barking like a man handing out orders on a deck. The ship is leaving for Singapore, the captain is mad, and the crew is a list of drunks and strays, all moving at once. Keith Richards plays guitar on the album, but nothing in this track sounds polished.

Waits hit the pots, pans and anvils himself, and the whole thing is mixed loud and close, as if the band were in the room with you. It does not apologize for a note of it.
