Pink Moon
Nick Drake — 1972
Listen: Pink Moon — Nick Drake

The title track runs two minutes and forty seconds, just guitar and voice until a single piano line — overdubbed by Drake himself in a spare hour at the studio — drops in near the end almost as an afterthought. He and engineer John Wood made the whole record alone over two nights in October 1971, no producer, no other musicians; twenty-eight minutes across eleven songs, the shortest and starkest of his three albums. When it was finished, Drake carried the tapes to Island Records' office himself and left them at reception without telling anyone he'd been there. The cover — a pink moon sliced open to show a cheese-rind face, painted by a friend of his sister's — sold almost nothing on release. He died less than three years later, and the sleeve's queasy cheerfulness reads differently now than it must have then.
