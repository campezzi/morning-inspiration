Tokyo Story
Yasujirō Ozu — 1953

The old couple travel from a small coastal town to Tokyo to see children who no longer have time for them, and the film never raises its voice about it. Ozu keeps the camera fixed at knee height, so a train platform or a doorway fills the whole frame the way it would to someone kneeling on a tatami mat. Only Noriko, their dead son's widow and no longer family by any legal claim, makes room for them — everyone else finds a reason to be busy. Near the end Shukichi asks her, plainly, "Isn't life disappointing?" and she says yes, still smiling. The mother's death happens off-screen, between two shots of an empty room, and nothing swells to meet it.
