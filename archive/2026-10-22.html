Monaco, 1979
Gilles Villeneuve, Ferrari 312T4 — 1979

The car is slightly sideways and the marshals are already looking past it, which is how Monaco in 1979 worked: the Armco is a hand's width from the front-left tyre and nobody in the picture is flinching but the driver's head is tilted into the corner like he's listening for something. Villeneuve's most famous drive from that year came at Zandvoort, where a rear tyre let go and he kept going, three wheels and a flailing corner of suspension, a full lap back to the pits. He wasn't trying to win by then. He just didn't have a setting for stopping.

Scheckter took the title in the other 312T4; Villeneuve finished second and is the one people still talk about.
