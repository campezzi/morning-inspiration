Start of the 1935 French Grand Prix
Unknown (Match Hebdo) — 1935

Four cars on the grid for the start of the 1935 French Grand Prix, left to right: Hans Stuck's Auto Union, Nuvolari's Alfa Romeo, Caracciola's Mercedes, Varzi's Auto Union — the shot ran in Match Hebdo that June. Nuvolari's transmission failed fourteen laps in, though he'd already posted the day's fastest lap. A month later at the Nürburgring, the same rivals lined up again, nine German cars against his one aging Alfa, and he stayed with them anyway — on the final lap Manfred von Brauchitsch punctured while leading, and Nuvolari went through. The organizers hadn't thought to bring a recording of the Italian anthem; Nuvolari had one in his own luggage.
