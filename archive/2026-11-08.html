Der Staat
Alfred Kubin — 1900

A state, drawn as a machine, rolls through an empty meadow on three iron drums. Its sliced-open side shows a small crowd in top hats and uniforms on a balcony, someone at a gun on the platform below, and a curved blade at the front like a plough. On the roof a few tiny figures walk along the edge, and nothing in the drawing says who is steering.

The grass in front is untouched, and the smoke behind is already a wall. Kubin made it at about twenty-three, and the machine looks too heavy to turn around.
