Bruder Klaus Field Chapel
Peter Zumthor — 2007

A farmer named Hermann-Josef Scheidtweiler built this with his wife and neighbors, out of gratitude, on a field above their village. Peter Zumthor waived his fee and designed a room formed by 112 tree trunks stacked into a tepee, then concrete poured around them over twenty-four working days. Once the shell had cured, a fire was lit inside and kept smouldering for three weeks, until the trunks had dried enough to pull free, leaving their charred grain pressed into the walls. The floor is lead, ladled on molten by hand. Rain still falls through the triangular opening where the trunks used to meet, and no one has ever fixed that.
