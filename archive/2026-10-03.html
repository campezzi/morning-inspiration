Jim Clark in a Lotus, Dutch Grand Prix
Harry Pot / Anefo — 1963

Clark is tucked down into the cockpit with the nose already pointing at the next corner, number 27 on a car that is barely more than a green cigar with wheels. Harry Pot shot it from trackside at Zandvoort in June 1963, close enough that the frame can't hold the whole car and doesn't try.

Nothing here is staged; it is one more lap, taken at speed, by someone standing very near the edge of it. Clark won seven of the ten championship rounds that year, and in this frame he looks like a man merely getting on with it.
