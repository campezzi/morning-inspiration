The Critic
Weegee — 1943

Weegee staged this one — planted a woman off the street outside the Metropolitan Opera on opening night and waited for the real show to arrive. Two grandes dames sweep past mid-stride, done up in ermine and tiaras, noticing nothing; the woman beside them is already sneering, and the whole picture turns on that one unguarded look. He shot it the way he shot everything that decade — Speed Graphic preset to ten feet, f/16, flashbulb, no time to think twice. It hit the wires within days and has been reprinted every few years since, the joke never needing an update.
