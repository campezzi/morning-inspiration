The Passion of Joan of Arc
Carl Theodor Dreyer — 1928

Falconetti's head is shaved for the role, no wig, no makeup — Dreyer wanted nothing between the camera and her face. In this frame, near the end, the camera tips up at her from below, the stake already planted behind her, a placard nailed to the wood. The original negative burned in a fire soon after release; the print every restoration since has used turned up decades later in a janitor's closet at a psychiatric hospital outside Oslo. It was Falconetti's last film — she never acted in front of a camera again.
