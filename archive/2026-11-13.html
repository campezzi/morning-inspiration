Carte du Tendre
Madeleine de Scudéry (engraved by François Chauveau) — 1654

Three rivers run down the map of Tendre, each ending in a town: Inclination, Esteem, Gratitude. The traveller is advised that the road through Esteem passes via Great Wit, Pretty Verses and Love Letters, which is to say the route to affection has been planned around a writing desk. Take the wrong turn and you reach the Lake of Indifference; overshoot, and you are in the Dangerous Sea, with the Unknown Land waiting on the far shore.

Madeleine de Scudéry drew it for her 1654 novel Clélie as a parlour game, and the engraver François Chauveau gave it the sober look of a real survey. Nobody in the salon, it seems, was fooled. They simply enjoyed having a scale bar for it.
