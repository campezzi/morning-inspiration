T3 Pocket Radio
Dieter Rams for Braun — 1958

Two pocket radios, side by side in a museum case, and only one of them is trying to be liked. On the left, the 1954 Regency TR-1 shows off with a gold dial and a cream shell; on the right, Dieter Rams's T3 for Braun answers with a pale grey box, a grid of speaker holes and a tuning ring with its numbers set around the edge like a very small, very serious clock.

It is a little funny that the plainer one is the one people say they want to live with. Nobody ever hung a pocket radio on the wall as a joke, but the T3 comes close, politely, in its own grey way. Look at the speaker holes: someone counted them, and then made sure the counting was a pleasure.

Source: Wikimedia Commons (Regency TR-1 left, Braun T3 right, LACMA)
