Scenographia Systematis Mundani Ptolemaici
Andreas Cellarius — 1660

At the center sits the earth, motionless, while the sun and five known planets loop around it on rings picked out in gold leaf — gold for bodies everyone in 1660 already knew didn't move that way. Cellarius drew the Ptolemaic system anyway, one cosmology among several competing ones in the atlas, because people had organized their lives around it for fourteen centuries and it was too handsome to leave out. Overhead, putti carry astronomical instruments through the sky; on the ground, scholars bend over globes and open books, measuring a heaven already superseded. A gold band of zodiac signs runs the whole way around, holding the picture shut like a frame.
