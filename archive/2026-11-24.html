Road in the Village of Baldersbrønde (Winter Day)
Laurits Andersen Ring — 1912

Nobody is in it. The road is glazed grey-blue with wheel ruts, the snow is pushed into heaps along the verge, and the only things standing up straight are a few thin trees with their buds already formed and nothing to show for them yet. Two cottages sit far off to the left, one with a yellow gable, their windows dark.

Ring painted the village in Zealand where he lived, and he came back to it in winter again and again. The sky takes up two-thirds of the canvas and the light along the horizon is the only warm thing in the picture, low and not going to last the afternoon.
