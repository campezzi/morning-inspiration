Fun House
The Stooges — 1970
Read about it: Fun House — The Stooges

The album opens on a drum beat that doesn't change for the length of "Down on the Street," and Iggy Pop comes in sounding like he's already halfway across the room. They cut it in Los Angeles in 1970, set up as if on stage, and producer Don Gallucci had them play the songs live until the takes were rough enough.

Steve Mackay's saxophone tears through the closing track, "L.A. Blues," until it stops being a song. The album sold badly, the band was dropped, and it was left to be found later by people who had been waiting for it without knowing.
