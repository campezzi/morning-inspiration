Evening on Karl Johan Street
Edvard Munch — 1892

A crowd walks straight at you down Oslo's main street, faces pale as plates, eyes wide and fixed on nothing. The coats are black and the lit windows on the left are a sour yellow, and the whole street seems to lean toward the viewer. At the right edge a single dark figure walks the other way, away from the crowd, and it is the only one who looks at ease.

Munch painted it in 1892, and it is hard to find the evening in it. Nobody is speaking to anyone.
