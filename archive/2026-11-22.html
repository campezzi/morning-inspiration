Chapel of Faith
Sigurd Lewerentz — 1925

A flat wall of sandstone blocks, a single louvred window, and below it a pair of bronze doors with two small figures pressed into them. The doors are the only thing on the facade that asks anything of you, and they are shut. The path to them is just wide stone slabs, with grass cut straight along the edges.

The sign beside the gate reads Trons kapell, the Chapel of Faith, one of the small chapels Sigurd Lewerentz drew for Stockholm's Woodland Cemetery in the 1920s. Nothing here announces grief; the building simply stands at the right distance from you and waits. The green streaks running down the bronze are what rain does over a hundred years.
