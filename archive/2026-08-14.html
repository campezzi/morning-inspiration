Fell in Love with a Girl
The White Stripes — 2001
Listen: Fell in Love with a Girl — The White Stripes

One minute fifty seconds, 192 beats per minute, cut in three days at Easley-McCain with Meg White saying afterward the songs had needed more practice. It doesn't sound like it needed anything: her drums stutter and crash while Jack's guitar just keeps chasing, no chorus, only his own voice doubling itself into a wordless hook after each verse. Michel Gondry built the video out of actual Lego bricks, frame by frame, after the band couldn't land an official partnership and went and bought the sets themselves. It's over before you've settled into it.
