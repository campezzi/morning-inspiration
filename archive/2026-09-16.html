The Chinese Restaurant
Seinfeld — 1991
Watch: The Chinese Restaurant — Seinfeld

Jerry, George, and Elaine spend the entire episode not getting a table. Elaine, starving, offers a stranger fifty dollars for one of his egg rolls and walks back with nothing but confusion. George loses the pay phone twice, the second time to a woman he never even sees coming. NBC wanted the episode killed before it aired — nothing happens, the executives said — and Larry David threatened to quit rather than soften it. Nothing happening turned out to be the whole point.
