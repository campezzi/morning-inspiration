Sixtyten
Boards of Canada — 1998
Listen: Sixtyten — Boards of Canada

Sixtyten runs five minutes and forty-eight seconds, buried sixth on Music Has the Right to Children, and never rises above a murmur. Boards of Canada — brothers Michael Sandison and Marcus Eoin, who kept that fact from journalists for years to avoid comparisons to Orbital — built the album on intentionally detuned synthesizers, drum machines, and reel-to-reel tape, so pitches drift like a memory losing its edges. They grew up making cassettes in the Pentland Hills and destroying most of them; what survived became something closer to weather than music. The track fades out rather than ending, the way most things people miss actually do.
