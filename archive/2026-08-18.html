Smokestack Lightning
Howlin' Wolf — 1956
Listen: Smokestack Lightning — Howlin' Wolf

"Smokestack Lightning" barely has lyrics — a handful of lines about a passing train, repeated until they stop functioning as words. What holds the record together is one droning guitar riff, unchanged for nearly three minutes, and Wolf's voice riding over it: half moan, half holler, dropping into a growl and climbing back out. Sam Phillips, who'd recorded Wolf a few years earlier in Memphis before he moved north to Chess, said hearing him was "where the soul of man never dies." No bridge, no key change, no release — just the riff and the howl, looped past the point where it resembles a song and starts to feel like weather closing in.
