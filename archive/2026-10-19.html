Street Advertising
John Thomson — 1877

Two bill-stickers are pasting up a notice on a Cheapside wall, one on a short ladder, the other holding his paste bucket and watching. Directly over their heads, in letters a foot high, a sign announces "World-Wide Circulation." Neither man looks up. The sign is advertising something else entirely, and the poster going up beneath it has been half-covered by the next one before the paste is dry.

From Street Life in London, John Thomson and Adolphe Smith · image via Wikimedia Commons
